How Entriq works

01

Verify the organisation

Submit organisation, banking, governance and required raffle classification evidence.

02

Build and publish

Configure tickets, prizes, dates, eligibility, terms, payment rules and promotional links.

03

Sell and reconcile

Issue digital tickets, monitor payments, reconcile funds and export operational reports.

04

Draw transparently

Close inventory, run an auditable draw, verify winners and retain evidence for review.
